Using Educational Data: Abu Bakr Mukhtar Speaks at ILAC

A look inside an international conference where data meets teaching — and how one English coach made the case for evidence-based decisions in education.

Conference Talk Learning & Development Data-Driven Teaching

About the conference

The ILAC international conference brought together educators, trainers, and education leaders to explore how modern teaching is evolving. Sessions focused on practical strategies that improve real learning outcomes — from classroom tools to institutional decision-making.

Abu Bakr's role

Abu Bakr Mukhtar joined as a speaker to share his perspective as an English teacher, a Learning & Development and Quality Assurance specialist, and a trainer who helps other educators grow.

His talk centered on one core idea: educational data is only useful when it is understood. He walked the audience through how teachers can collect meaningful data from learners' performance, how to read that data accurately, and how to turn it into decisions that genuinely improve learning.

Because his lesson design is supported by AI-powered learning materials, he also showed how data can guide personalization — letting each learner move at the right pace with the right support.

Data is not about numbers. It is about understanding, then deciding with confidence.

Key themes of the lecture

  • Collecting meaningful data from learners' performance
  • Understanding what the data really says
  • Making decisions based on evidence, not guesswork
  • Using data to personalize lessons for each learner
