Shadowing Practice

Listen to the model phrase, repeat it aloud right after, then record yourself and compare. The fastest way to train your accent.

How it works: draw a phrase → press Listen → speak at the same moment you hear it → press Record → compare yourself with Listen to My Voice.
Microphone is not available in this browser. You can still listen to the model phrases and repeat them out loud.

Tip: record your best phrase and send it to your teacher on WhatsApp for personal feedback.
